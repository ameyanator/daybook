Copy these into Play Console. Graphics are in this folder.

## Main store listing
- **App name** (30 max): Daybook
- **Short description** (80 max): Your day, one line at a time. A simple schedule you can share.
- **Full description** (4000 max):

Daybook is the simplest way to keep track of what's happening and when.

Pick a date and time, write one line about it, and Daybook puts everything in order for you, grouped by day. Things that don't need a time, like birthdays and trips, can be marked "all day".

• One line per thing. No forms, no clutter.
• Everything sorted by time and grouped into Today, Tomorrow, and the days ahead.
• Past items fold away so what's next stays front and centre.
• Share your schedule, read-only, with family or friends. Add them by email or send an invite link.
• See the calendars others share with you, side by side with your own.
• Sign in with Google. No passwords to remember.
• No ads. No tracking. Delete your account and all your data at any time from inside the app.
